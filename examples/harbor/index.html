    04 · Questions

    Before you come in.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Cards, loans, events and the tool library are free. Late returns are free too; we just ask you to bring things back.

    Can I borrow without a card?

    You can read anything in the building. Taking things home needs a card.

    Is the building accessible?

    Yes: step-free entrance on Quay Lane, a lift to both floors, and a hearing loop at the front desk.

    See you on Quay Lane.

    Accessibility at Quay Lane

    Step-free entrance on Quay Lane, with an automatic door.

    A lift serves both floors. Accessible toilets are on the ground floor.

    A hearing loop is fitted at the front desk. Assistance dogs are welcome.
